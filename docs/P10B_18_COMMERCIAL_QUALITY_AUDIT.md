## 1. Purpose and boundaries

This is the living P10B-18A/B/C record. P10B-18A audits the commercial quality ceiling of the
current registered authority before P10B-18B changes that authority and before P10B-18C runs the
100+ quality-and-diversity gate.

The audit uses deterministic, server-owned authority only. It makes no OpenAI or Vesko call and
does not publish. It preserves one `StorefrontSnapshot`, BrandSystem/Design DNA authority,
PageBlueprint registry, component registry, frame authority, dynamic-commerce authority,
product-card authority, renderer family, semantic compiler path and lifecycle.

The central counting rule is:

```text
registry identity
!= compatibility
!= exact selection
!= materializer consumption
!= renderer-visible structure
!= commercially meaningful visual variety
```

Fingerprints, IDs, customer copy, palette changes and catalogue cardinality are supporting
lineage. None is accepted alone as structural or visual variety.

## 2. Executive diagnosis

The current architecture is capable of producing complete, coherent and materially different
storefronts across Premium Editorial, Modern Technical and Minimal Commerce. Its commercial
ceiling is now limited by the reusable source authority rather than the provider, proposal
transport, canonical snapshot, lifecycle or renderer equivalence.

The decisive audit finding is more specific: five exact synthesis postures are represented in
selection and diversity fingerprints but are not consumed as independent materializer inputs.
Holding the compatible backbone constant while changing `narrativePosture`,
`merchandisingPosture`, `informationDensityPosture`, `artDirectionPosture` or `responsiveMode`
changes the synthesis fingerprint and generated identities while leaving Design DNA, site map,
frame, profiles, component choices, PageBlueprint overrides, assets, dynamic-commerce selection
and bounded parameters unchanged. These factors therefore cannot be counted as five additional
visual axes.

Static registry and renderer inspection identifies four shared-frame compositions, four
substantive dynamic PDP branches and five canonical product-card anatomies. The completed browser
review narrows that positive result: all 108 captures are complete, responsive and unclipped, but
each six-store direction cohort renders one fixed DNA, 65/72 matrix cases belong to repeated
topology clusters, and two different Premium Aurum intents produce six byte-identical surface/width
pairs. Approved assets materially improve the four Aurum stores; the other 14 selected stores
cannot fill presentation roles from canonical product media alone. The principal ceiling is
therefore the combination of non-consuming semantic postures, correlated DNA/frame/profile
selection, media-required compositions in asset-poor contexts and weak cardinality adaptation—not
provider transport, protected commerce, canonical media preservation or responsive correctness.

### 5.3 Why Cartesian counts are rejected

The factorized solver correctly avoids materializing a posture Cartesian product. However, its
factor options still inflate theoretical diversity because five posture choices do not alter the
materializer contract for a fixed backbone. P10B-18A therefore retains an exact consumed-authority
signature for lineage: Design DNA direction/narrowing, shared frame, included page/profile
authority, PageBlueprint overrides, approved asset assignments and dynamic-commerce selection.
That signature can still change with route cardinality, page keys, asset identity and product-type
mappings, so it is not itself a structural-variety count.

A separate clean structure/topology signature excludes route and catalogue cardinality, page keys,
merchant/catalogue/product-type IDs, copy, palette alone, asset IDs/URLs/fingerprints, request seed,
generated IDs and decision/diversity fingerprints. It retains frame, normalized profile/component/
variant/anatomy membership keyed by family/slot, material layout parameters, Design DNA structural
categories, responsive transformations and placement/crop modes. The helper deliberately sorts
and deduplicates those membership sets, so this signature does not preserve their order or
multiplicity. Ordered homepage composition is reported separately as its own matrix metric.
Component choices and bounded parameters remain profile-derived descriptors, not independent exact
axes.

Candidate eligibility also must not be called winner reachability. The current resolver can score
63 Karvonen or 114 Aurum backbones, but an exhaustive proof that every eligible candidate can win a
truthful semantic request does not exist. Card anatomy, component variant and bounded parameters
are profile-derived rather than independent candidate dimensions. Optional page-set choice has no
provider scoring field and otherwise resolves through stable tie-breaking.

## 9. Template and future-canvas readiness

A future curated template can remain a thin, versioned constraint bundle of existing Design DNA,
frame, PageBlueprint profiles, archetypes, component variants, bounded parameters and asset-role
expectations. It must compile and fingerprint through the current solver/materializer and must not
contain merchant IDs, component implementations, snapshots or asset IDs. The three current
page-local legacy recipes are not sufficient complete-template authority, but no second template
engine is required.

The canvas boundary is also sound: `StorefrontSnapshot` and `PageModel` remain canonical, Puck data

canvas operations can address stable page, section, frame, profile, component and archetype
identities while representative commerce/search contexts remain transient; accepted operations

## 10. Scalability diagnosis

The factorized solver is metadata-bounded, but it does enumerate a Cartesian backbone across nine
registered direction/profile/frame dimensions before filtering. The current raw inventory is 864
tuples and is guarded by `MAX_COMPATIBLE_COORDINATED_DIRECTION_CANDIDATES = 8_192`. It does not
multiply or materialize the five synthesis posture factors into that backbone. The semantic
influence audit also performs pairwise comparison across its retained samples, so the current 504
Karvonen and 912 Aurum samples imply quadratic comparison work in that diagnostic even though
materialization remains bounded. Design-authority cardinality is otherwise independent of product
count; dynamic route inventory and canonical catalogue projections grow with catalogue size.
Derived maps/search indices remain transient and fingerprint-bound.

P10B-18A does not make tenant or production-scale claims. Audit risks for later measurement are:

- repeated linear route/catalogue scans during dynamic route resolution;
- repeated full-catalogue fingerprints in some render/adapter paths;
- relationship/facet selection that must remain one-pass as product types grow;
- capability growth multiplying compatibility evaluation if new frames/profiles are treated as a
  raw Cartesian inventory instead of factorized constraints.

| State class       | Current authority                                                                                                              |

| Merchant-specific | Project, BrandSystem/Design DNA value, approved brief/evidence/assets, catalogue, snapshot and lifecycle records               |
