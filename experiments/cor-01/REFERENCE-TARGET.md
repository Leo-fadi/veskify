Status: proposed evaluation target, **not owner visual acceptance**. Catalogue/photography approval is separate. Preserve its technical evidence, do not reproduce its structure or polish it here. A technical PASS cannot confer commercial acceptance.

## References inspected on 20 October 2026

Two relevant retailers were inspected in a fresh browser at 1440 and 375 px, covering home, collection and product pages. Screenshots remain private in `COR-01/references-01`; no retailer images, code or copy enter this experiment. These are specific strengths to learn from, not whole-site endorsements.

- **Tom Wood:** [home](https://www.tomwoodproject.com/), [necklaces](https://www.tomwoodproject.com/collections/necklaces), [Curb Chain Slim](https://www.tomwoodproject.com/products/curb-chain-slim). Selected for jewellery scale and disciplined merchandising. The collection uses consistent image areas, almost no card containers, compact names/prices, and visible filter/sort entry points. Mobile keeps two columns and readable metadata. The PDP moves from a large image beside a focused choice panel to image then choices on mobile. The home establishes a single photographic idea before related product paths. Do not borrow its branding, products, imagery, claims or oversized full-screen photography. Lower home images were lazy-loaded and absent from the first full-page capture; those blank areas are not evidence of intentional whitespace.
- **Ferm Living:** [home](https://fermliving.com/), [cups](https://fermliving.com/collections/cups), [Toky Cup](https://fermliving.com/products/toky-cup-cream). Selected for a non-jewellery transfer: category links connect inspiration to shopping; consistent object photography makes unlike shapes comparable; collection cards and PDP distinguish availability. Mobile category navigation becomes horizontal and the collection retains two columns; product controls and details stack. Cookie/newsletter overlays obstructed several captures, including a broken mobile overlay. The unobstructed collection/PDP captures support these observations; do not emulate those interruptions or claim flawless mobile UX.
